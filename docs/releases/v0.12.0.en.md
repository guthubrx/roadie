# Roadie 0.12.0 - Go back to where you clicked

October 30, 2026 · [Français](https://github.com/guthubrx/roadie/blob/v0.12.0/docs/releases/v0.12.0.md)

![Roadie's zone switcher](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guthubrx/roadie/v0.12.0/docs/assets/screenshots/zone-switcher.png)

## The zone switcher (Option+Tab)

Cmd+Tab brings back a window. Roadie now brings back **a place inside a window**: the button you pressed, the cell
you were editing, the message you were reading. Every place you click becomes a zone, with a small photo. Hold
Option and press Tab: recent zones show up as cards, grouped by desktop and stage, with all of Cmd+Tab's behavior
(arrows, mouse, search, Escape). Release: Roadie brings the window back, switches stage or desktop if needed, and
clicks the zone again. Two Option+Tab in a row go back and forth between your last two places.

- Zones come from your clicks (3 per window) and from on-screen search; kept 24 hours, 20 at most.
- Photos are retaken when you leave a window, at most every 10 seconds; they never leave your Mac.
- A yellow border on the real app: the chosen zone while you pick, then where the click landed.
- Hovering a card: × removes the zone (or closes the window in Cmd+Tab), the no-entry sign excludes an app.

**Getting started**: Settings > Switcher > Shortcut profiles, add a profile, choose "Content: Recent zones" and a
built-in shortcut, for example `alt+tab`. Without a zones profile, Roadie records no zone and no photo.

## On-screen search, separate from the launcher

Its own shortcut (`screen_search_hotkey`, for example Capslock + F) opens the bar **without a list**: type a word
visible on screen, its elements get a label, Return or Shift + label clicks. **Typos** are forgiven when nothing
matches exactly ("enregsitrer" finds "Enregistrer"), on screen and in the launcher ("Did you mean" section). The
launcher can stop reading the screen (`launcher_on_screen`).

## Alfred actions in the bar

- **Files** (one or several): copy or move to a folder, open with, email, Trash, alias, Finder's Get Info window,
  tags, browse, terminal, package contents.
- **Typed or selected text**: copy, web search, extract addresses, email, large type.
- **Tabs and apps**: close, copy the address, relaunch, quit others, pin in Apps.
- `menu …`: the frontmost app's menus; dark mode, screenshot and Apple Shortcuts commands.
- Alfred 6 features: `apps` grid, SF Symbols browser (`symbols`), glass themes, Markdown tables.
- ⌘, ⌥ and ⌃ + Return can be set.

## Floating windows

A floating window that opens under the rail is moved to its right (`floating_avoid_rail`), and a rule can make it
come back to its last place, position and size (`remember_frame`).

## Fixes

- A workflow Text view without an input field crashed Roadie.
- `roadie window explain 0` stopped the daemon.
- A click on an on-screen element was refused right after the bar closed; Shift + label sent a Shift-click.

## Updating

Existing configuration and shortcuts are kept; new features stay off until you set them up (zones profile,
on-screen search shortcut). This version requires **macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon**. Download `Roadie.dmg` and
`Roadie.dmg.sha256` from the [0.12.0 release](https://github.com/guthubrx/roadie/releases/tag/v0.12.0), then check
the package:

```bash
shasum -a 256 -c Roadie.dmg.sha256
```

The package is ad hoc signed and not notarized by Apple. Zone photos use the Screen Recording permission already
requested for the rail thumbnails.
